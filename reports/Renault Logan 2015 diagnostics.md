# Scan first, then decode the Logan's computers

A 2015 Renault Logan II (DDT4ALL project X52, Renault-badged L52) with the K7M 1.6 8v and JH3 manual gearbox has a small, predictable network. All its diagnosable modules sit on **one diagnostic CAN bus at 500 kbps (OBD pins 6/14)**. The engine ECU answers at 7E0/7E8, ABS at 740/760, airbag at 752/772, the UCH body computer (which also handles the immobiliser) at 745/765 and the cluster at 743/763. DDT4ALL and pyren agree on all of these addresses ([DDT4ALL](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all); [pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2)). An ELM327 app can reach every module, provided the adapter supports Renault-style flow control (`ATFCSH/ATFCSD/ATFCSM1`) and the app opens the Renault session with `10C0` before identifying each unit with `2180`. The hard part is not transport but meaning. Renault DF fault numbers, live-data local IDs and actuator commands are defined per ECU software version in the DDT2000/CLIP database, which DDT4ALL's GitHub repository does **not** ship. Public sources also disagree on basic facts: which engine ECU is fitted (Continental EMS3120/EMS3132 vs Siemens SIM32), injector DF numbering, and airbag crash-code numbering. The app should therefore discover modules and identities at runtime and treat every DF table here as a seed to verify, not ground truth. Generic Mode 01 data and the cooling fan, fuel pump and lamp tests are realistic, low-risk features. Clearing airbag crash data, ABS bleed or valve routines, keys, VIN, odometer and flashing must be blocked at the protocol level. The fastest way to close the remaining gaps is one DDT4ALL scan of the actual car with a DDT2000 database loaded, saving every module's `2180` identification and DTC dump.

Confidence labels used throughout: **C = confirmed** by at least one cited Logan, Logan II or same-platform (Largus EMS 31.32) source; **P = probable**, consistent with Renault conventions but not confirmed for this car; **X = conflicting**, where sources disagree; **U = unverified**, from a non-Logan source, a low-trust aggregator or engineering inference. None of the "confirmed" items has yet been checked on this specific Egyptian car.

## Eleven CAN addresses cover every module the car can have

### The vehicle is X52, and its modules share one 500 kbps bus

DDT4ALL and pyren classify Renault platforms by project code. **X90 is Logan I / Sandero I (2004–2012) and X52 is Logan II / Sandero II (2012–2020)**; "L90/L52" are the Renault-badged variants sold in markets such as Egypt, with the same diagnostic addressing. Renault Egypt says the Logan was "completely restyled in 2014" ([renault.com.eg](https://renault.com.eg/cars/logan/overview.html)), so a 2015 car should be X52. Still confirm from the VIN, or by seeing which address table answers, because Logan I used K-line as well. pyren's vehicle table lists X90 with ISO K/L-line on pins 7/15 plus CAN on 6/14, while **X52 has no K-line at all**: CAN1 on 6/14, plus a CAN2 on 13/12 on which nothing is expected for a K7M car ([pyren-OBD2 README](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2)). That simplifies the app: one protocol (`ATSP6`, 11-bit, 500 kbps), with a fallback to 250 kbps (`ATSP8`), because pyren supports "double BRP" ECUs that need it.

The table below merges DDT4ALL's `projects.json` X52 entry with pyren's `mod_elm.py` and function-to-address map ([DDT4ALL](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all); [pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2)). Both tools list identical IDs for X52 and X90. A note in the research brief that placed ABS at Renault address 28 is wrong: **ABS is address 01**, and 28 is "CSHV" (78A/7D7). One secondary note also gave the airbag as 772/792 and reversed the ABS pair. The code-derived values below take precedence.

The hardware column is the weakest part of the picture. A tuning-tool catalogue lists **EMS3120** for Logan/Sandero and **EMS3132** for Logan/Sandero/Symbol/Largus ([ecutools.eu](https://ecutools.eu/chip-tuning/chiptuningpro), snippet only). A repair blog describes a no-start Logan with a Continental EMS3132 ([obdii365](https://blog.obdii365.com/2024/08/14/how-to-repair-renault-logan-ems3132-ecm/)). A Renault fault-finding document for Logan/Sandero/Thalia 2 instead describes a **Siemens SIM 32/SIM 321** 112-track injection computer, with the immobiliser wired to the UCH ([Scribd fault summary](https://www.scribd.com/document/401418049/Fault-summary-table), excerpt). The likeliest explanation is that both are right for different years and markets. The app does not need to know in advance, because the `2180` reply carries supplier, part number, software version and Vdiag, and those select the data dictionary. A reset-tool vendor's list of "Temic OBDII Logan 2" as an SRS type ([diagcode](https://www.diagcode.com/products/airbagtool/)) and Continental "A2C" part numbers on Sandero airbag units ([crashdata.co.uk](https://crashdata.co.uk/product/a2c17351701-dacia-sandero-airbag-module-repair-and-reset-98510332r/)) point to a Continental airbag ECU. Parts catalogues list both hydraulic and electro-hydraulic steering pumps for Logan II ([autopartspro](https://www.autopartspro.co.uk/carparts-online/power-steering-pump/dacia-logan/logan-ii)), so whether address 04 answers is itself a useful finding.

### The ELM327 sequence needs flow control and a Renault session

Renault "DiagOnCan" is KWP2000 carried over ISO-TP. pyren and DDT4ALL configure each module the same way: `AT SH <tx>`, `AT FC SH <tx>`, `AT FC SD 30 00 00` (block size 0, STmin 0), `AT FC SM 1`, `AT ST FF`, `AT SP 6` (or `AT SP 8` for 250 kbps), `AT CRA <rx>`, with global init `AT E1/L0/H0/D0`, `AT S0`, `AT AL` and `AT CAF0` for manual PCI framing. DDT4ALL probes the bus by sending the raw single frame `0210C0`, then identifies with `2180` (positive reply `61 80…`). For UDS-style ECUs it uses `1003` and reads `22F1A0`, `22F18A` (supplier), `22F194` and `22F195`. pyren keeps the session alive by re-sending `10C0` after **4 s** of silence rather than `3E` ([pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2); [DDT4ALL](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all), `elm.py`, `ecu_scanner.py`). DDT4ALL also re-applies `AT CAF0/S0/AL` after `AT SP`, because some clones reset formatting on protocol change, and it logs "received first frame only… FC failed" when they do. A minimal per-module sequence for the app, using ABS as the example, follows directly from that code:

```
ATZ  ATE0  ATS0  ATH1  ATAL
ATSP6            (fallback ATSP8 = 250 kbps)
ATCAF0           (re-send after ATSP on clones)
ATSH740  ATCRA760
ATFCSH740  ATFCSD300000  ATFCSM1
0210C0           -> expect 50 C0   (Renault diagnostic session)
022180           -> 61 80 ...      (multi-frame ident; app reassembles 10/21/22... frames)
... re-send 10C0 every <4 s while the module is open
DTCs: try 1902AF, fall back to 17FF00 ; clear: 14FF00 (per ECU definition)
```

For DTC reading, pyren limits `1902xx` to `1902AF` on non-OBDLink adapters, because 38,400 baud is "not enough to read full information about errors". It also falls back to `AT CFC0` (software flow control) when it sees buffer-full errors. A Zoe owner's clone ELM hit **"BUFFER FULL"** reading about 30 UCH codes, and the fix was `ATCFC0` or an STN-based adapter ([DDT4ALL discussion #1081](https://www.github.com/cedricp/ddt4all/discussions/1081)). Adapter choice is a product decision, not a footnote. pyren requires "ELM327 adapter with FlowControl support (original one or chinese v1.5)". FORScan's forum reports that many "v2.1" clones implement "half of original ELM327 commands" ([FORScan forum](https://forum.forscan.org/viewtopic.php?p=5601)). DDT4ALL lists OBDLink SX/EX as "tested and confirmed" and vLinker FS as the most stable. pyren's README claims Bluetooth ELMs fail on Android 7+ and recommends WiFi. That conflicts with the everyday experience of Android Bluetooth OBD apps and is probably specific to pyren's Python stack, so test both. At connect, the app should probe `ATI`, `AT@1`, `ATSH`, `ATCRA`, `ATFCSH/SD/SM`, `ATCFC0` and `ATCAF0`. If any return `?`, it should drop to generic-OBD-only mode and disable every write.

### Generic OBD reaches only the engine; everything else needs the DDT2000 database

Mode 01/02/03/04/07/09/0A over 7DF or 7E0 works only on the engine ECU. ABS, airbag, UCH and cluster faults, and the engine's Renault-specific parameters, need KWP-on-CAN services. These are `21xx` ReadDataByLocalIdentifier for live data, `17FF00` or `1902xx` for DTCs, `14FF00` to clear, and `30`/`31` for actuators. **DDT4ALL's repository contains the address tables but not the per-ECU definitions.** It loads them from `ecus/eculist.xml` plus ECU XML files, or from a converted `ecu.zip` with `db.json`, supplied separately from the DDT2000 database ([DDT4ALL](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all), `ecu_database.py`). pyren lists X52 as supported in both its CLIP-database and DocDB modes ([pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2)). Without those definitions an app can still scan modules, identify them, and read and clear raw DTC bytes. It cannot name the faults or decode ABS, airbag or UCH live data.

## Fault codes belong to an ECU, not to the car

### The database key must be ECU family, software and DF number

The most important design rule from the research is that **Renault DF numbers are per ECU**. DF006 in the ABS and DF006 in the UCH are unrelated faults. Even the same module type renumbers its codes between ECU generations. Secondary sites that mix DFs across ECUs produce contradictions. One lists DF007 as both "rear left wheel speed sensor" (ABS) and "rail pressure sensor" (injection) ([obdii.pro DF007](https://obdii.pro/en/code/DF007)). The fault database should be keyed on (module, supplier/software/Vdiag from `2180`, DF number), with the OBD P/C/B/U equivalent stored as a secondary field. Severity legend for the app: **S = safe to drive (آمن للقيادة)**, **W = fix this week (أصلح خلال أسبوع)**, **X = stop now / do not drive far (توقف فورًا)**. These grades are engineering judgement, not Renault classifications.

### Engine ECU (K7M, EMS31xx-family): about 20 usable codes, with three numbering conflicts

The best seeds are a Logan/Sandero DF-to-P table ([sanderologan.ru](https://sanderologan.ru/logan/diagnosticheskie-kody.html)) and the Lada Largus EMS 31.32 tables. The Largus 8v uses the same K7M and EMS3132 package ([autoruk.ru](https://autoruk.ru/en/lada-largus/ecu-fault-codes-and-troubleshooting-for-lada-largus); [largus-mcv.ru](https://largus-mcv.ru/html/neispravnosti-esud.html)). The Largus tables set a priority rule worth hard-coding: **deal with DF587 (+5 V sensor supply) and DF514 (fuel pump relay circuit) first** when either is present. The Logan table appears to merge K7J, K7M and K4M ECUs. It includes electronic-throttle (DF009, DF078) and VVT (DF080) codes that a cable-throttle, no-VVT 8v K7M cannot set, so the app should label those "not applicable to K7M 8v". The engine uses a **cable throttle with an idle stepper (RXX) on the throttle body**, driven by three ECU lines ([auto.ru logbook](https://auto.ru/logbook/cars/renault/logan/6065201/); [drive2 pinout](https://www.drive2.ru/l/6091914/)). The engine-control diagram names a crankshaft sensor (B54) and coolant sensor (B24) but no camshaft sensor ([remavtocar.ru](https://remavtocar.ru/renault_logan_upravlenie_dvigatelem.html)).

### ABS: wheel-speed sensors dominate, and the DF list is unknown for Logan II

The only Renault-sourced ABS DF is from Bosch 8.1 documentation: **DF006 "front left wheel speed sensor circuit"**, to be handled first when several faults are present ([Scribd fault summary](https://www.scribd.com/document/401418049/Fault-summary-table), excerpt). Logan II rear sensors are **active (Hall/magnetic-encoder) sensors**, about 440 mm long, with separate left and right parts ([autodoc](https://www.autodoc.parts/car-parts/abs-sensor-10138/dacia/logan/logan-ii/58612-tce-90)). One listing mentions a 26-tooth rear ring, which conflicts with the encoder-in-bearing design ([autopartspro](https://www.autopartspro.co.uk/carparts-online/abs-sensor/renault-logan/logan-ii)). Active sensors read a magnet in the bearing and can be damaged if the bearing is replaced with the sensor still fitted ([NTN-SNR](https://www.NTN-SNR.com/sites/default/files/2023-01/Wheel%20speed%20sensors_EN.pdf)).

The U0121 causes are from [RepairPal](https://repairpal.com/obd-ii-code-u0121). Rust on rear tone rings or encoders is a very common cause of intermittent ABS lights, and a new sensor will not cure a rusted ring ([apextechnation](https://apextechnation.com/articles/abs-warning-light)). The severity split rests on standard ABS/EBD behaviour, not a Dacia source: an amber ABS lamp alone means normal braking remains, while ABS plus the red brake lamp means EBD may be lost. Confirm this against the owner's manual.

### Airbag: clock springs fail most often, and crash records cannot be cleared

On Renault SRS systems the common faults are the driver-airbag loop through the clock spring and loose or corroded connectors. A Mégane example logged **DF071/DF072** (driver airbag circuit 2/1 open) that returned after clearing ([Car Mechanics via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/car-mechanics-uk/20190801/283562337123109)). Repairers advise checking the plug at the ECU and the clock-spring-to-ECU wiring before buying a spring ([vehicleservicepros](https://vehicleservicepros.com/12167402)). Crash handling is the critical conflict. Renault airbag fault-finding says **"It is prohibited to use command RZ001 Clear fault memory when DF001 Computer is present or stored"**, and the remedy is replacing the computer ([Scribd Renault airbag](https://www.scribd.com/document/609392295/Renault), excerpt). An airbag-repair vendor claims **DF194 = "crash data saved"** on Sandero II ([airbag24](https://www.airbag24.de/en/products/dacia-sandero-ii-airbag-steuergeraet-reparatur)), and a Mégane II Autoliv unit used DF034 "computer locked" ([digital-kaos](https://www.digital-kaos.co.uk/forums/showthread.php/295489-Airbag-crash-in-Renault-Megane-II-Autoliv)). The app should block the airbag clear whenever any "computer/crash/locked" DF is present, in whichever numbering the ECU uses. Separately, the Logan II/Sandero/Lodgy/Dokker steering-wheel airbag 985701142R is under recall for 2018 production ([car-recalls.eu](https://car-recalls.eu/fr/inscription/dacia-logan-2018-3/)). That does not cover a 2015 car, but a replaced wheel may carry that part.

### UCH, cluster and network: thin public data, one Logan-specific clue

UCH fault numbering is poorly documented. A low-trust aggregator lists UCH DF001 internal, DF002 steering lock, DF003 clutch switch and DF004 brake switch ([obdii.pro](https://obdii.pro/en/code/DF007)). Clio-family sources give DF066 "multiplexed network" and DF130 "instrument panel not configured" ([renaultforums](https://www.renaultforums.co.uk/threads/clio-iii-1-4-16v-immobiliser-issue-fixed.576082/page-2)), and a Sandero II thread links DF280 to the immobiliser ([mhhauto](https://mhhauto.com/Thread-Dacia-Sandero-II-2013)). The one Logan-specific fact is valuable: **on the Logan, the UCH and the diagnostic socket share one fuse**, and an aftermarket alarm tapped into it caused UCH communication faults and a persistent DF044 ([daciaclub.ro](https://www.daciaclub.ro/Logan-computer-error-DF044-t38815.html), snippet; DF044 meaning unconfirmed). The UCH reads the key transponder and sends an authorisation code to the engine ECU. Flat key batteries and wet or damaged key chips are common no-start causes ([startmycar](https://www.startmycar.com/dacia/logan/guides/immobilizer-anti-theft-system)), and the manual says only a dealer can repair the immobiliser ([Dacia manual](https://www.user-manual.dacia.com/en/getting-to-know-your-vehicle/engine-immobiliser)).

## Generic PIDs cover the dashboard the Logan lacks

The 2013–2020 Dacia Sandero/Logan generation has **no coolant temperature gauge, only an over-temperature warning lamp**. Owners fit an ELM327 to see coolant temperature ([daciaforum](https://www.daciaforum.co.uk/threads/no-temperature-gauge.8185/); [HonestJohn](https://www.honestjohn.co.uk/forum/post/index.htm?t=107947)). That makes PID 0x05 the app's single most valuable feature for an Egyptian owner. Newer Dacia manual pages describe a coolant reading on the trip computer, but they apply to Logan III or other clusters ([Dacia manual](https://www.user-manual.dacia.com/lv/node/1797)). Do not promise a cluster page, and record on the car whether the Renault-badged L52 cluster differs. No source lists which Mode 01 PIDs the K7M ECU supports, and SAE J1979 leaves support optional ([CSS Electronics](https://www.csselectronics.com/pages/obd2-pid-table-on-board-diagnostics-j1979)). The app must read the 0100/0120/0140 bitmasks and show only PIDs that answer.

| PID | Parameter | Expected on K7M | Normal (warm, healthy) | App use |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Only the 90 °C and 850 rpm values are sourced. The other "normal" values are generic petrol-engine expectations to confirm by logging a healthy car. Renault-specific parameters (CLIP "PR" values and "ET" statuses) need the ECU's DDT definition. Expected names include idle stepper position/RCO, idle regulation adaptive, richness adaptives at idle and under load, fan low/high command, A/C request and immobiliser status. Read them with `21xx` local IDs (or `22xxxx` on UDS-calibrated ECUs) after `10C0`. No public K7M definition was opened. A Lodgy K9K diesel Torque PID file shows the format ([gdincu](https://www.github.com/gdincu/DaciaLodgy-K9K-OBD2-PIDs)) but has no K7M content. A DDT4ALL fork includes a `Renault_Logan_2G.docx` covering extra functions on Logan 2G, mostly MediaNav-oriented ([dimoroz772/DDT4ALL](https://github.com/dimoroz772/DDT4ALL)). The fan thresholds widely quoted for this ECU (low speed ~99 °C, high ~102 °C, warning ~118 °C, thermostat ~89 °C) are **unverified**. Aftermarket thermo-switch data (92 °C on, 82 °C off) belongs to switch parts, not to the ECU-controlled fan ([autopartspro](https://www.autopartspro.co.uk/carparts-online/radiator-fan-switch/dacia-logan/logan-iii)). Measure them on the car by logging PID 05 against the fan relay click.

## Relearns are mostly driving, not commands

The K7M has no electronic throttle, injector coding or VVT, so most "relearns" happen on their own. Injector coding discussions for Dacia concern only the 1.5 dCi K9K ([PlaneteRenault](https://www.planeterenault.com/forum/codage-injecteurs-dacia-sandero-1-5-dci-68-ch-t56883.html?amp=1)). After throttle-body or stepper cleaning or a battery disconnect, the ECU relearns idle. A Largus K7M/EMS3132 owner cured a ~2,000 rpm cold idle by reconnecting the battery with the ignition on and letting the stepper recalibrate ([lada-largus.com](http://www.lada-largus.com/forum/thread6781-1.html)). Generic guidance adds that high idle after cleaning usually comes from an air leak or disturbed hose, and that a weak battery interrupts adaptation ([iCarsoft](https://icarsoft.com/knowledge/article/throttle-body-relearn-guide); [YourMechanic](https://www.yourmechanic.com/question/high-idle-after-throttle-body-cleaning)). The app's guided procedure has five steps. Clean the throttle body and stepper with the engine off, without touching the stop screw. Switch the ignition on for 10–30 s without starting. Idle without touching the pedal until the fan cycles once, with all loads off. Then run the A/C for a few minutes. Finally, drive normally while the trims settle. This sequence is best practice to confirm, not a Renault procedure. A forced adaptive reset is a Renault "RZ" command whose number and bytes for this ECU were not found. Only RZ007 (clear faults) is confirmed in a Logan/Sandero document ([Scribd fault summary](https://www.scribd.com/document/401418049/Fault-summary-table)). Flywheel/TDC target learning on Renault petrol ECUs typically happens during decelerations, which is unverified for K7M.

The official **service-interval reset** needs no OBD. With the ignition on and engine off, show "distance before service" and hold the trip button about 10 s until the value is steady. Reset both service and oil distances where both exist ([Dacia manual](https://www.user-manual.dacia.com/sk/node/1796)). Logan Mk2 videos show the button on the end of the right-hand stalk and a 20,000 km interval ([scegliauto](https://www.scegliauto.com/en/video/dacia/tutorial/39067/)).

Published service intervals conflict, and no Renault Egypt booklet was found, so the app should default to severe-service values and let the owner override them. auto-abc gives **60,000 km** for the Logan 1.6 petrol timing belt ([auto-abc](https://www.auto-abc.eu/Dacia-Logan/v20377-2007/service)), an aggregator gives 120,000 km / 6 years ([onlinerepairmanuals](https://onlinerepairmanuals.com/dacia/logan/)), and Autodoc's "timing chain" for the K7M is almost certainly an error for this belt-driven engine ([Autodoc](https://www.autodoc.co.uk/car-parts/timing-chain-10511/renault/logan/logan-express/59105-1-6)). Because weak K7M water pumps are linked to overheating and head-gasket failure ([AutoHints](https://www.autohints.com/renault-k7m-16l-90hp-inline-4-cylinder-engine-12966)), the pump belongs with every belt job, and 60,000 km / ~4 years suits Egyptian heat and dust. YallaMotor's Arabic advice is oil every **5,000–7,500 km** or yearly and an air filter about every 15,000 km ([YallaMotor](https://www.yallamotor.com/ar/news/top-repair-and-maintenance-tips-for-the-2025-renault-logan-mcv-46637)). The **JH3** gearbox takes about **2.8 L of Elf Tranself TRJ 75W-80** and has no scheduled change ([avtotachki JH3](https://en.avtotachki.com/mehanicheskaya-korobka-renault-jh3/); [cararac](https://cararac.com/gear_oil/dacia/logan.html)). The remaining figures are unverified background to confirm from the booklet: oil capacity ~3.3–3.5 L to RN0700, plugs ~20,000–30,000 km (shorter on CNG), Glacéol RX Type D coolant, and DOT4 brake fluid every 2 years.

## Egypt's heat, traffic and CNG shape the real fault list

Across K7M reviews, five problems recur. The first is a **cracked ignition coil pack** that lets moisture in and misfires ([mymotorlist](https://mymotorlist.com/engines/renault/k7m/)). The second is a **crank/TDC sensor or its oxidised connector**, which gives a car that cranks but will not fire when hot; wiggling the wiring is the quick test ([dailydriven.ro](https://dailydriven.ro/en/renault-k7m-16-8v-engine-specs-issues-reliability-1592247)). The third is a **weak water pump leading to overheating and head-gasket failure**, with milky oil and coolant loss without a leak ([AutoHints](https://www.autohints.com/renault-k7m-16l-90hp-inline-4-cylinder-engine-12966)). The fourth is a **dirty idle stepper or throttle body** causing hunting idle and stalling at junctions ([enginescope.gr](https://enginescope.gr/engine/renault-k7j-k7m/)). The fifth is **speed-sensor wiring** that kept one owner's MIL on through three diagnoses ([StartMyCar](https://www.startmycar.com/gb/dacia/logan/problems/dashboard)). Autodoc adds clogged injectors and weak fuel pumps after 70,000–90,000 km ([Autodoc blog](https://www.autodoc.co.uk/info/problems-with-the-dacia-logan)), while K7M reviews call the low-pressure injectors tolerant of poor fuel. The two can be reconciled: deposits build slowly but rarely kill the injector. StartMyCar's Logan overheating list is fan, thermostat, low coolant and pump ([StartMyCar](https://www.startmycar.com/dacia/logan/guides/overheating)). In Cairo traffic at 40 °C, that makes the fan circuit (DF018/DF019, relay, resistor, CTS) the single most consequential fault family, and the fan test the most useful actuator.

CNG conversion is common in Egypt. An Ain Shams University study notes "many technical problems" in petrol cars converted under the national CNG push ([ASU](https://research.asu.edu.eg/handle/123456789/203545)), and SAE work links CNG's dry, hot combustion to faster valve and seat wear ([SAE 2016-01-1089](https://saemobilus.sae.org/content/2016-01-1089)). No Logan-specific CNG fault data was found. The diagnostic implications are inference. Fuel trims shift, often lean with positive LTFT, on gas. Misfire codes rise because gas needs higher ignition voltage from coils and plugs. Faulty injector emulators can set P0201–P0204-type circuit codes. The app should log trims separately for petrol and gas, flag CNG before interpreting lean codes, and shorten plug and coil intervals. Egyptian Arabic forums returned no indexable Logan technical content, so the Egypt ranking rests on climate logic plus international owner reports.

## Control actions: offer the fan, block the brakes and airbag

### What can be commanded, and how risky it is

Both pyren and DDT4ALL can run ECU-defined "equipment tests" and configuration changes over ELM327, with explicit warnings. pyren says "BE VERY CAREFUL. ONLY YOU RESPONSIBLE FOR ALL YOU ARE DOING WITH YOUR CAR", and DDT4ALL says that in Expert mode "serious damage is possible on a live vehicle" ([pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2); [DDT4ALL](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all)). The real-world failures are instructive. A Logan III owner ended up with dashboard warnings, lost functions and "NO DATA" on every ECU ([DDT4ALL #1631](https://github.com/cedricp/ddt4all/discussions/1631)), though Logan III has a secure gateway that Logan II lacks. A Sandero owner hit cluster firmware problems ([PlaneteRenault](https://www.planeterenault.com/forum/sandero-cherche-1-backup-firmware-calc-sandero-ii-phase-1-t58535.html?amp=1)), and a flat battery mid-session caused trouble ([DDT4ALL #1089](https://www.github.com/cedricp/ddt4all/discussions/1089)). Specialist recovery products exist for bad Sandero II UCH configurations ([Abrites RR031](https://abrites.com/partial/rr031)). The service bytes below come from ISO 14230/14229 conventions, not a fetched Logan definition. KWP uses `30` IOControlByLocalIdentifier, `31/32` routines and `3B` write; UDS uses `2F`, `31` and `2E`. The local ID for each actuator must come from the ECU's DDT definition.

The UCH configuration path is documented. Renault MR426 shows configuration in Repair mode with ignition on, including **CF272 single-door opening** ([Scribd MR426](https://www.scribd.com/document/512930490/MR426B9087B000), excerpt), and the Dacia manual already exposes "lock doors when driving" as a user setting ([Dacia manual](https://www.user-manual.dacia.com/el/node/1802)). That is a reason to prefer the car's own menu over a CAN write. Key and UCH work is sold as separately licensed specialist functions ([Abrites RR014](https://abrites.com/partial/rr014)), which confirms it belongs outside a consumer app. On the legal side, Egypt's traffic-law amendment fines EGP 5,000–15,000 for violations including exhaust that breaches environmental standards ([Egypt Independent](https://www.egyptindependent.com/?p=2816363)), and the executive regulation of Environment Law 4/1994 bars over-limit vehicles ([UNEP LEAP](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/eg/national-legislation/decree-no-338-1995-issuing-implementing-regulation-environment)). No Egypt-specific odometer statute was found, though Hatla2ee treats clocked odometers as a known used-car fraud ([Hatla2ee](https://eg.hatla2ee.com/news/200833/كيف-تكتشف-عداد-الكيلومترات-المزيف-في-ا/)).

Enforcement should be a **service-ID whitelist**, not a blacklist. Allow `10` default/Renault `C0`/extended, `3E`, `14`, `17/18/19`, `21/22`, and a curated list of (ECU-version, `30/2F/31` ID) pairs. Reject everything else, including `27`, `34–37`, `3D`, `28`, `85`, `11` beyond a soft reset, and programming sessions. Every actuator test should hold `10C0` or `3E` keep-alive, auto-stop on a timer, and send an explicit return-to-ECU on exit or reconnect. A dropped Bluetooth link should end the test when the ECU session times out after a few seconds, which is unverified per ECU. That is acceptable for a fan but not for ABS valves. Every write should be logged with timestamp, ECU, request and response.

### "Connect with DDT4ALL": reuse its map, import its database, keep its dangerous parts out

DDT4ALL is a desktop tool, and the owner's goal of reading everything and controlling as much as possible maps onto it in three layers. First, the app can reimplement DDT4ALL's transport: the X52 address table, the `ATFCSH` setup and the `10C0`/`2180` scan. Those are public in its repository, but check its licence before reusing code. Second, the meaning layer comes from the DDT2000 database. DDT4ALL reads it from a user-supplied `ecu.zip`/`eculist.xml`, and it is not in the repository, so the realistic Android design is an **import** feature. The app matches each module's `2180` reply to the right ECU XML, then renders its parameters, DTC texts and screen buttons, exposing only whitelisted buttons. Third, the immediate practical step for this owner is to run DDT4ALL or pyren on a laptop with a FlowControl-capable adapter, scan the car, and save identifications, DTC dumps and the parameter list for each detected ECU. pyren can save, compare and roll back configuration dumps in DDT mode ([pyren-OBD2](https://github.com/emtee40/pyren-OBD2)). That one session converts most of the U and X rows above into confirmed rows.

## Why the gaps exist and what closes them

This research could read source code but few manuals. The DDT4ALL and pyren repositories were cloned and inspected, which is why the protocol layer is solid. Most other hosts were blocked or failed to resolve in the research environment: Scribd (Renault MR388/MR426/MR453 fault-finding manuals), autoruk.ru, renaultbook.ru, sanderologan.ru, daciaclub.ro, mr-fix.info, auto.ru, startmycar, mymotorlist, Wikipedia and FAOLEX. Many engine and body findings therefore rest on search-result snippets, not full pages. Russian, Romanian and Egyptian Arabic owner forums with Logan II code histories were not readable. No public K7M parameter file was found. Three things would close nearly every gap. In order of value: a DDT4ALL or pyren scan of this car with the DDT2000 database loaded; access to the Renault MR fault-finding manuals for X52 sections 17B (injection), 38C (ABS), 88C (airbag) and 87B (UCH); and the Renault Egypt service booklet.

## Conclusion

The research shifts where the difficulty lies. Addressing the Logan's modules is solved: two independent open-source tools agree on every CAN ID, and the ELM327 recipe is concrete. What is unsolved is knowledge that lives only in Renault's per-ECU database. The public internet holds a patchwork of DF tables from sister ECUs, and their contradictions (DF017 diesel vs petrol, three injector numberings, three crash-code numbers) would mislead a user if hard-coded. The app's architecture should follow from that: discover modules, identify ECU versions, and treat every meaning as a lookup keyed by ECU version, never as a constant.

For an Egyptian owner, the highest-value product sits in the safe zone. That means a live coolant gauge the car itself does not offer, fan-circuit diagnosis and a fan actuator test for summer traffic, fuel-trim tracking that understands CNG, and severe-service reminders for belt, pump, oil and plugs. "Control as much as possible" should become "control what fails safely". Timed, engine-off, self-reverting outputs (fan, pump, purge, lamps) can be offered, while anything touching brakes, airbags, keys, VIN, odometer or calibration is refused at the service-ID level, regardless of what an imported DDT screen exposes.
